# celPix

A cross-platform graphics editor for retro games. It reads tiles, palettes,
tilemaps and text straight out of a ROM — compressed or not — lets you edit them
where they are, and writes them back in place.

- **[Getting Started](Getting-Started)** — a first project from an unopened ROM
  to an edited overworld written back, on Super Mario World: a palette, four tile
  sheets and the map drawn through them.

Every page works on the same cartridge, and each builds a piece of one project.

- **[Text and Fonts](Text-and-Fonts)** — a font sheet, its alphabet, and the
  message boxes read and typed as words.
- **[VRAM Windows](VRAM-Windows)** — composites: gaps held open, and pieces cut
  out of other sheets.
- **[Pinned Palette Rows](Pinned-Palette-Rows)** — a sheet shown in several
  palette rows at once.
- **[Plugin Inputs](Plugin-Inputs)** — formats that keep part of themselves
  somewhere else in the file.
- **[Compress & Reshape](Compress-and-Reshape)** — maps the game rearranges
  after it unpacks them.
